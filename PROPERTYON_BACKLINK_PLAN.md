# PropertyON Saudi Backlink and Digital PR Plan

Objective: earn relevant references that help Saudi real-estate office operators discover and trust PropertyON. No paid bulk links, PBNs, comment spam or automated directory blasts.

## High-value opportunities

### Saudi proptech and real-estate publications

- Pitch practical, non-promotional pieces on office operations, rent follow-up and contract-data quality.
- Offer real screenshots and workflow explanations, not fabricated market statistics.
- Link destination: the most relevant commercial or educational page, not always the homepage.

### Industry partnerships

- Property-management consultants, training providers and implementation partners.
- Co-create an office-operations checklist or migration guide.
- Require editorially relevant links and transparent attribution.

### Founder/operator content

- Publish first-hand lessons about building Arabic-first real-estate operations software.
- Useful channels: Saudi SME/proptech interviews, podcasts and founder communities.
- Link to a deep workflow page when the discussion supports it.

### Link-worthy tools

1. Rent-schedule calculator.
2. Contract-expiry calculator.
3. Saudi property-office operations checklist.
4. Excel rent-follow-up template plus migration guide.
5. Property expense tracker.

These assets should work without registration. A light PropertyON CTA can follow the useful result.

## Medium-value opportunities

### Curated SaaS and proptech directories

- Submit only to maintained directories with relevant Saudi/GCC audiences.
- Use accurate early-access descriptions and the canonical homepage.
- Do not invent a company address, founding story, customer count or integrations.

### Guest educational content

- Topics: overdue-rent workflow, receipt documentation, contract expiry and property reports.
- Avoid duplicated articles and exact-match anchor requirements.

### Community resources

- Share genuinely useful checklists/templates in property-management communities where promotional links are permitted.
- Do not automate posting or repeat the same message.

## Low priority

- General global startup directories with no Saudi or B2B relevance.
- Unmoderated link pages.
- Coupon, press-release syndication and “top software” list purchases.
- Any site offering guaranteed ranking or hundreds of links.

## Outreach qualification score

Score each opportunity 0–2 on:

- Saudi/GCC audience relevance.
- Real-estate operations relevance.
- Editorial quality.
- Estimated referral value.
- Ability to link to a genuinely useful asset.

Pursue scores of 8–10 first, 5–7 selectively and reject below 5.

## Five initial pitches

1. “كيف ينتقل المكتب العقاري من جداول متفرقة إلى سجل تشغيلي مترابط؟”
2. “لماذا لا يكفي تسجيل التحصيل دون ربطه بالدفعة والسند؟”
3. “قائمة مراجعة بيانات عقد الإيجار قبل إدخاله إلى نظام المكتب.”
4. “خمسة تقارير يحتاجها مدير المكتب العقاري لمتابعة التشغيل.”
5. “طريقة عملية لمراجعة الوحدات الشاغرة والعقود القريبة من الانتهاء.”

## Governance

- Maintain a contact, pitch, destination, status and earned-link sheet.
- Never exchange undisclosed payment for an editorial endorsement.
- Review earned links quarterly for relevance and broken destinations.
- Do not use confidential product or customer data in outreach.
