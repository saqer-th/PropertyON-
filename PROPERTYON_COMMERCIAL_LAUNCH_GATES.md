# PropertyON Paid Commercial Launch Gates

Created: 19 August 2026  
Current launch mode: `EARLY_ACCESS / PRE-LAUNCH`

The items below are **not complete**. They must be resolved before enabling or publicly presenting paid commercial subscriptions. The current Privacy Notice and Website & Early Access Terms do not close these future gates.

| Gate | Status | Required outcome before paid launch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `REGISTERED_CONTRACTING_ENTITY` | OPEN | Establish and approve the registered contracting entity, official name, CR, applicable VAT/tax details, and public address. |
| `FINAL_PRIVACY_LEGAL_REVIEW` | OPEN | Obtain formal review and approval of the final commercial-service privacy policy. |
| `PDPL_ROLE_AND_LEGAL_BASIS_REVIEW` | OPEN | Confirm controller/processor roles and the legal basis for each material processing purpose. |
| `PROCESSING_LOCATION_REVIEW` | OPEN | Confirm production processing/storage locations, international-transfer posture, and required disclosures or safeguards. |
| `RETENTION_AND_DELETION_POLICY` | OPEN | Approve record-category retention periods, deletion/destruction rules, preserved-record exceptions, and backup handling. |
| `DATA_SUBJECT_REQUEST_PROCEDURE` | OPEN | Establish verified intake, identity/authority checks, routing, response handling, exceptions, and evidence keeping. |
| `FINAL_COMMERCIAL_TERMS` | OPEN | Approve complete paid SaaS terms and the process for presenting and accepting them. |
| `PRICING_AND_VAT` | OPEN | Approve prices, billing cadence, taxes/VAT treatment, invoicing identity, and public presentation. |
| `CANCELLATION_POLICY` | OPEN | Approve cancellation method, effective date, access consequences, and notices. |
| `REFUND_POLICY` | OPEN | Approve eligibility, exclusions, timing, and operational handling of refunds. |
| `TERMINATION_TERMS` | OPEN | Approve suspension/termination rights, customer consequences, data access/export, and transition behavior. |
| `LIABILITY_TERMS` | OPEN | Obtain legal approval for warranties, disclaimers, responsibility allocation, and any liability cap. |
| `GOVERNING_LAW_AND_DISPUTE_PROCESS` | OPEN | Approve governing law, venue/courts, and any dispute-escalation or arbitration process. |
| `PROCESSOR/VENDOR_REVIEW` | OPEN | Verify production processors/vendors, agreements, security/privacy due diligence, subprocessors, and public disclosures. |
