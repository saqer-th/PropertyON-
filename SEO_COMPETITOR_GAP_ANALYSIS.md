# PropertyON Saudi SERP Competitor Gap Analysis

Research date: 22 August 2026. Results are directional snapshots; rankings vary by location, device and personalization.

## SERP summary

Arabic commercial searches are dominated by vendor homepages rather than focused workflow pages. That creates an opening for PropertyON to publish intent-specific pages with real product proof. Government/Ejar content appears when queries include contracts, payment or Ejar, so PropertyON must clearly distinguish office operations from official government services.

## Competitor review

### Osool Proptech — https://www.osoolproptech.com/

- Page type: deep Saudi platform homepage.
- Title/positioning: program for Saudi property offices; combines assets, contracts, collection, accounting and owner reports.
- Depth: extensive feature, pricing and enterprise sections.
- Trust: Saudi-market claims, ZATCA/Mada/Apple Pay/FAL references, product UI and clear package limits.
- Pricing: visible and prominent.
- Internal linking: solutions, resources, pricing and enterprise paths.
- Intent match: strong for broad software and office queries.
- Gap for PropertyON: Osool is broad and commerce-heavy. PropertyON can be more precise about the operating chain and show exact collection and review workflows without premature pricing or unsupported integrations.

### Moajir — https://moajir.com/

- Page type: long Arabic-first product homepage with section anchors.
- Positioning: Saudi operator workflow around Ejar, ZATCA and WhatsApp.
- Depth: high; problem framing, contracts, extraction, automation, compliance, pricing and FAQ.
- Product proof: UI-like visuals and detailed workflow language.
- Trust: strong local terminology, but many claims are broader than PropertyON’s verified scope.
- Internal linking: contract, invoicing, agency and compliance pages.
- Intent match: excellent for Ejar PDF and “without spreadsheets” searches.
- Gap for PropertyON: PropertyON has verified Smart Import evidence/confidence/human-review captures and can own the careful, non-integration framing. It should not copy Moajir’s ZATCA, WhatsApp automation, predictive AI, data-residency or pricing claims.

### Mogod — https://www.mogod.sa/

- Page type: broad feature-led SaaS homepage with multiple solution links.
- Positioning: one program for properties, contracts, collections, invoices and reports.
- Depth: broad coverage across Ejar, ZATCA, automation, portals, maintenance and approvals.
- Product proof: dashboard image and feature sections.
- Trust: specific integrations and Saudi hosting statements.
- Internal linking: many feature-detail pages.
- Intent match: strong for broad property-management queries.
- Gap for PropertyON: Mogod’s breadth can make narrower buyers work harder to verify a workflow. PropertyON should lead with one real screen and one operational story per page.

### Aqartix — https://aqartix.app/

- Page type: lightweight Arabic product/app homepage.
- Positioning: property management for Saudi landlords and offices.
- Depth: concise feature list, three-step flow, pricing/free claim, reviews and app CTA.
- Product proof: limited compared with the breadth of claims.
- Trust: Arabic-first copy and familiar operational terms; testimonial evidence is not independently established by the SERP page.
- Internal linking: limited.
- Intent match: good for receipts and mobile-app searches.
- Gap for PropertyON: stronger real screenshots, no fabricated reviews, and deeper receipt/collection context.

### Yarn — https://yarn.com.sa/ar/

- Page type: broad product/automation homepage.
- Positioning: comprehensive property-management automation.
- Depth: large feature inventory including maintenance, online payment and portals.
- Trust: quantitative claims and broad capability statements.
- Gap for PropertyON: a narrower office-operations story with visible qualifiers can feel more credible for buyers who need contracts, collection and reporting rather than a full facility suite.

### Owner.sa — https://www.owner.sa/ar

- Page type: modern product homepage.
- Positioning: units, tenants, contracts and payments for owners, developers and managers.
- Depth: concise.
- Gap for PropertyON: stronger targeting of the office as buyer, deeper Arabic workflow content and verified captures.

## Cross-SERP findings

| Dimension | Common competitor behavior | PropertyON response |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic copy | Often strong at homepage level, weak or absent on workflow pages | Publish original Arabic pages around distinct office problems |
| Saudi context | Ejar, ZATCA, FAL and local payment claims | Mention Saudi office context but claim only supported PDF review, SAR and verified local workflows |
| Product visuals | Mix of real screens, mockups and decorative dashboards | Use only approved Marketing Demo captures with descriptive alt text |
| Pricing | Frequently prominent | Preserve early access and no public pricing until commercial gates close |
| Proof | Reviews, counts and integration badges are common | Do not invent proof; use exact workflow behavior and screenshots |
| Information architecture | Broad feature menus and multiple solution pages | Use ten focused pages and three contextual links per page |
| Schema | Varies; often generic Organization/Product markup | Use truthful WebPage, BreadcrumbList and global SoftwareApplication only |
| FAQ | Common and often sales-oriented | Answer real buyer boundaries visibly; omit low-value FAQ schema |

## Where PropertyON can win

1. **Connected operating model:** explain why property, unit, contract, schedule, collection, receipt, expense and report must retain their relationships.
2. **Collection detail:** show due/overdue/partial/completed states and oldest-first allocation without implying online payment.
3. **Smart Import honesty:** supported Ejar-family PDF → extraction → evidence/confidence → human review; explicitly no official Ejar integration.
4. **Office buyer focus:** write for office owners and teams rather than landlords, tenants, buyers or listing seekers.
5. **Real screenshots:** each commercial page uses a unique qualified capture or crop.
6. **No fake trust theater:** no ratings, customer counts, “best” claims, invented integrations, residency statements or testimonials.

## Content-quality bar

A PropertyON page should be more useful than a competitor feature card by answering:

- What office problem causes the search?
- What is the exact record or workflow in PropertyON?
- What happens before, during and after the action?
- How does it connect to another record?
- What does PropertyON deliberately not do?
- What should the buyer review next?
