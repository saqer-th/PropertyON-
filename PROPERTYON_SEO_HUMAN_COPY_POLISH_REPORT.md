## What changed across every page

- Removed the visible internal-strategy card built around `المشتري / النية / الدليل`.
- Replaced it with three page-specific customer outcomes written for Saudi real-estate offices.
- Replaced internal or defensive UI phrases such as `نطاق المنتج الحالي` and `قدرات موثقة...` with useful customer-facing guidance.
- Added a contextual label to each real product screenshot.
- Gave each page its own related-solutions heading and natural destination-card title.
- Gave each page its own FAQ heading and introduction.
- Rewrote internal marketing commentary such as “this page targets...” or “this page does not market...” into direct product language.
- Kept the primary H1, title, meta description, slug, canonical, schema, sitemap membership, and internal-link destinations unchanged.
- Updated the stable content-review date to `2026-08-23` because visible copy changed.
