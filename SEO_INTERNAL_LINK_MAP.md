# PropertyON SEO Internal Link Map

## Principles

- Every indexable commercial page is reachable from the homepage or another page within two clicks.
- Homepage expansion remains compact: five high-intent links, not a keyword directory.
- Each commercial page links to three genuinely adjacent workflows.
- Anchors describe the destination in natural Arabic.
- Legal pages remain legal destinations; they are not used as SEO hubs.
- `/v2-preview` receives no public internal links.

## Anchor safeguards

Avoid repeating the exact primary keyword more than context requires. Natural alternatives include:

- مساحة إدارة العقار
- متابعة العقود وجداول الدفعات
- دورة التحصيل والسند
- سجل الدفعات والتقارير
- تنظيم وحدات العقار
- الانتقال من الجداول إلى سجلات مترابطة
