## Terminology decisions

- `إدارة الأملاك` is the primary category phrase; use `إدارة العقارات` naturally as a synonym.
- `مكتب عقاري` is the strongest buyer qualifier and belongs in H1s, titles and CTAs where appropriate.
- `برنامج` should lead BOFU pages. `نظام` and `منظومة` are supporting synonyms, not repetitive title modifiers.
- `تحصيل الإيجارات` must be paired with recording, allocation and follow-up language to avoid implying a payment gateway.
- `عقد إيجار PDF` can mention Ejar-family documents, but the copy must explicitly deny official Ejar API integration or direct synchronization.
- `بوابة المالك/المستأجر` is not a target cluster because current access is relationship-scoped rather than a complete standalone portal.
