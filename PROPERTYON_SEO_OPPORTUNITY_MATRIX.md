## Interpretation

- Exact-office and workflow terms can outperform broad-volume phrases because they filter out tenants, buyers and listing traffic.
- Rent collection has the best differentiation score, but the page must state that PropertyON records and reconciles office collections and does not process rent online.
- Ejar PDF intent is strategically valuable even if volume is modest because the product has qualified, visible proof competitors often describe vaguely.
- Units and expenses are supporting commercial pages, not homepage-level positioning terms.

Week 3: pages 8–10 and full indexability QA.
